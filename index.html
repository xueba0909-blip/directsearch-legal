DirectSearch 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-10-17　运营者：Xiaolei Zhang

DirectSearch 没有账号，也不要求登录。

搜索关键词、历史、收藏、使用统计、平台开关、购买状态和免费体验的结束时间都保存在这台设备上。主 App 和桌面小组件通过系统的 App Group 读取同一份本地数据。

我们没有用来接收搜索内容的服务器，也不会把关键词上传给自己或第三方。不使用广告标识符，不接入第三方统计，不做跨 App 追踪。

购买由 Apple 处理。我们只通过 StoreKit 在本机确认你是否已经买断，并用 Apple 的签名校验这笔交易。

当你主动搜索时，关键词会出现在打开的链接里。那个链接会交给你选择的平台或系统浏览器，不是交给我们。

如果你打开「小组件显示最近搜索」，最近的关键词会显示在主屏幕上，任何能看到主屏幕的人都能看见。

反馈邮件只有在你点「反馈」并发送后才会离开设备。删除 App 会删除上述本地数据。

如有问题，请发邮件到 feedback@directsearch.app。

Privacy Policy (English)

DirectSearch has no accounts and does not require sign-in.

Keywords, history, saved searches, stats, platform switches, purchase status, and the free-month end date stay on this device. The app and the widget read that same local data through an App Group.

There is no server that receives search content. Keywords are not uploaded to us or to third parties. The app does not use the advertising identifier, does not include analytics SDKs, and does not track across apps.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We only confirm on-device, through StoreKit, whether you already bought the lifetime unlock, using Apple’s signed transaction.

When you search, the keyword appears in the link that opens. That link goes to the platform you chose or to Safari, not to us.

If you turn on recent searches on the widget, those words appear on the Home Screen and anyone who can see the Home Screen can read them.

Feedback email leaves the device only after you tap Feedback and send it. Deleting the app deletes the local data listed above.

Questions: feedback@directsearch.app.
